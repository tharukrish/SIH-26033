## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.
Develop a digital platform that directly connects farmers with consumers, retailers, supermarkets, hotels and other bulk buyers, reducing dependency on unnecessary intermediaries.

The platform will provide:
1. Direct Marketplace: Farmers can list and sell their agricultural products directly to buyers.
2. AI-Based Price Recommendation: Suggests fair selling prices based on market trends, demand and product availability.
3. Real-Time Market Prices: Provides updated agricultural market price information to help farmers make informed decisions.
4. Collective Selling: Enables small-scale farmers to combine their products and sell in bulk.
5. Location-Based Connectivity: Connects nearby farmers with consumers to reduce transportation costs.
6. Secure Digital Payments: Enables transparent payments directly to farmers.
7. Order and Delivery Tracking: Helps farmers and buyers monitor orders and deliveries.
8. Multilingual Voice Support: Supports regional languages to make the platform accessible to farmers.
Expected Impact:
- Increases farmers' earnings by reducing unnecessary middlemen.
- Reduces additional costs for consumers.
- Improves transparency in agricultural pricing.
- Provides wider market access to small-scale farmers.
- Reduces food wastage through better demand and supply management.
Innovation: The combination of AI-based price recommendations, collective selling, direct buyer connections and transparent pricing creates a smarter agricultural supply chain.

Final Objective: To build a transparent, affordable and efficient agricultural marketplace that ensures fair earnings for farmers and reasonable prices for consumers.

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:
FarmDirect – AI-Powered Agricultural Marketplace

FarmDirect is an integrated digital marketplace designed to connect farmers and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) directly with consumers and bulk buyers, reducing dependency on unnecessary intermediaries.

The platform provides three major functionalities: a direct digital marketplace for agricultural products, smart logistics support for transportation and delivery, and AI-powered demand forecasting and route optimization.

The AI system analyses historical sales, seasonal trends and market demand to predict future product requirements. Route optimization helps identify efficient delivery routes, group nearby orders and reduce transportation costs.

The platform also provides product listings, transparent pricing, digital payments and real-time order tracking.

Our objective is to improve farmers' earnings, make agricultural products affordable for consumers, optimize logistics and build a transparent, efficient and technology-driven agricultural supply chain.

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.
